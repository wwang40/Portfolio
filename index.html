About

Cornell '28

I'm a junior at Cornell pursuing a B.A. in Computer Science, most interested in building and optimizing pipelines — systems general enough to reuse across problems, specific enough to actually hold up: turning a photo of a jacket or a free-text query about where to move into something structured and fast.

Most recently that meant backend engineering at NYMA, where I built the pipeline that prices secondhand fashion listings from photos alone. Outside of that, I build things for fun — an immigration recommender, a reservation system, a Chrome extension full of AI pets.

Away from a keyboard, I row, play trumpet in the Big Red Marching Band, and collect unreasonably specific opinions about food pairings.
